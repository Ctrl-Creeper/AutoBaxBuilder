This directory contains a specification–behaviour consistency coding task. Please complete it.

Read INSTRUCTIONS.md first and follow it exactly. Work through tasks/T01.md … tasks/T12.md in file
order, and record your judgements in coder2_answers.json using answers_template.json as the shape.

Four things the instructions say that are easy to miss, so I am repeating them here:

1. Judgements are final once recorded. If a later task changes your mind about an earlier one, put
   that in the earlier task's `notes` field and leave the judgement standing. Do not go back and edit.
2. Within a task, finish every case in J1 before you read the witness implementation for J2.
3. "Determined" without a verbatim quote from the specification is invalid. When you cannot quote a
   sentence, the answer is "undetermined". Undetermined is the default when you are torn.
4. Do not classify, group, rank, or summarise the tasks. There is no category to assign and no total
   to report. Per-case and per-task judgements only.

Work only from the files in this directory. Do not search elsewhere for context about where these
tasks came from or what they are for — that context is deliberately withheld and looking for it would
invalidate the coding.

Take the tasks at face value: they are ordinary function specifications, and the question in every
case is only whether the specification settles what the function must do at that input.
