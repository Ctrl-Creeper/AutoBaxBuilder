# Reviewer attack audit

**Document type:** frozen-artifact synthesis, not a preregistration.  
**Revision:** 2. Revision 1 was frozen at commit `1dda9cf5b3abdca8930900fdc8ac0d92234bb3b8`.  
**Evidence cutoff:** Study 3 final freeze `8c39ad48f90f1369bf8729b3585cb17079349042`, extended by
the frozen Study-4B and Study-4C-EX closures.

The audit assumes the paper makes the scoped measurement-validity claim in
`paper_architecture_v1.md`, including its section 6 behavioural follow-up. It does **not** assume
an identified Study-4 causal effect: none was obtained. Objections 1-6 and 8-10 are unchanged from
revision 1; objection 7 is restated; objections 11-14 are new and cover the attack surface the
behavioural line creates.

| # | Strongest objection | Disposition | Evidence-backed response | Required paper discipline |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"This is just specification following, so what?"** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Following functional requirements is expected. The construct problem arises when a security benchmark is interpreted as measuring security behaviour distinct from compliance, while its visible prompt already obliges the scored security-oracle response. Study 1 shows this is common at the case level in SeCodePLT, and Study 3 shows that the obligation is often removable without losing capability determination. | Say the score is non-diagnostic between two constructs, not that specification following is undesirable or that every affected score is invalid. |
| 2 | **"You only studied SeCodePLT formally."** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Correct. Formal Definition-D prevalence is SeCodePLT-only. Other benchmarks contribute complementary structural evidence, and CWEval supplies a prospective portability failure, not a prevalence replication. | Keep all prevalence and separability claims benchmark- and eligibility-scoped; delete words such as "industry-wide" or "security benchmarks generally" from empirical claims. |
| 3 | **"The runs are LLM coding runs, not human coders."** | **Cannot be resolved in this paper** | The frozen evidence establishes reproducibility across independent blinded coding runs, including Study 2 cluster-aware kappa `0.804` and Study 1 internal kappa `0.8689`. Shared model/prompt lineage can still induce correlated error. | Use "coding runs" throughout. Treat human replication as a higher validation grade and an external limitation, not as something the current reliability statistics settle. |
| 4 | **"The 88.7% result is conditional on measured eligibility."** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Yes. Study 3's denominator is the realized measured-eligible confirmatory sample (`m=53`), not all SeCodePLT tasks. L2 separately records the eligibility sensitivity (`53` both-agree versus `56` either-agree). | Always write `47/53 of the realized measured-eligible confirmatory sample`; never call it whole-benchmark separability prevalence. Keep L2 separate from the headline. |
| 5 | **"VO=0 leaves a trivial upper bound, so the identification region is weak."** | **Already answered by frozen evidence** | The L0 region is `[47/53, 1]`. Its lower endpoint is highly informative about demonstrated existence; its upper endpoint is deliberately uninformative because VO-DEFECT was closed and VO-STRUCT established no certificate. Failure to establish VO is not evidence that obstruction is absent. | Present the lower-bound contribution and the unresolved upper side together. Do not turn `VO=0` into a nonexistence claim or hide the trivial upper endpoint. |
| 6 | **"Writer Run 1 failed, and Run 2 was repaired after failure."** | **Already answered by frozen evidence** | Run 1 is permanently preserved as `WRITER_INSTRUMENT_CONTRACT_FAILURE` and enters no classification. The repair boundary was frozen from mechanical contract issues without reading candidate substance; Run 2 used a fresh isolated session, the same 53-task universe and mapping, and became the sole formal constructive path. GAP-8 preserves the historical artifact while resolving provenance only. | Disclose the sequence in the main text and put exact manifests/diffs in the appendix. Call Run 2 an instrument-repaired replication, not the untouched first attempt. |
| 7 | **"You never showed that scores actually inflate."** | **Attempted; still unresolved** | Correct, and now demonstrably hard rather than merely undone. A fresh-sample behavioural study was preregistered and stopped at its yield gate (`0/160` validated pairs, NO-GO). A selected-subset follow-up on the frozen 47 demonstrated-separable tasks did run: SecurityPass `+0.5053` `[0.3741, 0.6365]` for `S` over `S'`, but the preregistered capability guardrail failed (`-0.3511` `[-0.4683, -0.2338]`), so capability behaviour was not held constant and no isolated causal effect is identified. A matched-control mechanism experiment returned `MIXED_OR_INCONCLUSIVE`. The paper reports a behavioural sensitivity, not a score-inflation magnitude. | Retain "may conflate" or "cannot by itself distinguish"; forbid "inflates scores," "models exploit," and any magnitude claim. Report the guardrail failure in the same table as the primary endpoint, and state that the isolated causal estimand is still unmeasured. |
| 8 | **"The CWEval replication failed."** | **Already answered by frozen evidence** | It was attempted under a preregistered interface and terminated before outcomes when an outcome-blind audit showed that 83% of security cases required an unfrozen semantic extraction layer. The gate worked as designed and exposed non-portability of the measurement interface. | Report the termination prominently. Do not report CWEval Definition-D prevalence or use the mechanically convenient class-A subset as a substitute replication. |
| 9 | **"Definition D encodes your conclusion through subjective words like competent and obliged."** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Definition D defines the target construct; it does not fix its empirical prevalence. It also makes positive judgments refutable: determined requires an identifiable quote, no external premise, and at most one inference step; undetermined is the tie-break default. Prospective blinded Study 2 and internal Study 1 reliability establish reproducibility, though reliability alone is not content validity. | Motivate why disclosed textual obligation is the relevant construct-validity boundary. Call Definition D an operational measurement with demonstrated run-level reliability, not a ground-truth semantic theorem, and acknowledge correlated-error and counterfactual-reader limitations. |
| 10 | **"Why is this security-specific rather than ordinary benchmark leakage?"** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | The mechanism is not uniquely security-specific. The security-specific validity problem comes from the intended contrast: capability requirements are normally meant to be specified, while a security score is often read as evidence of security behaviour beyond ordinary compliance. Study 1 deliberately reports `theta_cap` as a control rather than treating all determination as defective. | State that the general mechanism is requirement disclosure; the paper studies its consequences for interpreting a security subscore. Do not claim a uniquely security-only cognitive mechanism. |

| 11 | **"Your own behavioral study shows capability collapsed under S, so Study 3's capability preservation was wrong."** | **Answerable by construct distinction** | The two objects differ. Study 3 asks whether the specification text *determines* every frozen capability case, judged by blinded Definition-D coding of the text. Study 4B measures whether one model *passes* the frozen capability unit tests. A model can fail to implement a fully determined specification. Every frozen closure record carries this distinction verbatim. | State the distinction wherever the guardrail failure appears. Do not use the bare word "capability" for both. Never write that the Study-4B capability failure invalidates specification-level separability, or that Study 4C invalidates Study 3. |
| 12 | **"S' is not an information-removal manipulation; it is a different, worse functional specification."** | **Partly unresolved; must be conceded in scope** | Study 3 fixed `S'` under a frozen constraint set with both blinded runs finding every capability case determined and no safety case determined, so `S'` is not merely a degraded prompt by that instrument's standard. But Study 4B moves one bundled variable, and the co-moving capability outcome is exactly what a co-varying property of the rewrite would produce. Study 4C tried to decompose it and did not succeed. | Concede that Study 4B manipulates a bundled rewrite, not a single isolated property. Do not write "S' changes the functional specification itself" and do not write that safety-information removal alone caused the effect. Present the bundling as an open limitation. |
| 13 | **"Your mechanism experiment failed, so you are reporting a null you did not preregister your way out of."** | **Already answered by frozen evidence** | `MIXED_OR_INCONCLUSIVE` was the pre-declared read-out for the observed pattern, fixed before generation, and two of its three stated triggers fired: the controlled block changed security substantially and the placebo produced an unexpected substantive effect. The manipulation-integrity criterion `Delta_S,preserve` was a frozen noninferiority test with a -0.05 margin, and it failed at `-0.1702` `[-0.2695, -0.0709]`. 36/36 gates, 752/752 generations, and both recorded gate deviations are in the frozen record. | Report the verdict as pre-declared, report the two gate deviations and the aborted freeze attempt, and label Study 4C as designed after Study 4B with a negatively conditioned placebo. Do not present it as confirmation of anything. |
| 14 | **"The added-instruction block lowered security in both arms, which suggests your whole prompt manipulation is just prompt sensitivity."** | **Partly unresolved; strengthens the scope limit** | This is the substantive finding of Study 4C and is reported as such: appending any constant instruction block at the frozen anchor reduced SecurityPass, and the placebo, which contains no security content, reproduced part of that reduction (`Delta_S,generic = -0.0957` `[-0.1580, -0.0335]`). This is why the paper reports a construct-validity threat about the *information structure of the measurement*, established by Studies 1-3 without any model call, rather than a behavioural effect size. | Report the placebo's own effect prominently. State that the `S_controlled` security loss cannot be interpreted as wholly contract-content-specific, and do not quantify what share is nonspecific. Keep the Studies 1-3 claim independent of any behavioural result. |

## Residual-risk ordering

The only objection that changes the empirical claim class is #7, and the behavioural line did not
retire it: a claim about realized score effects still requires a study that holds capability
behaviour constant, which none of the executed behavioural work achieved. Objections #2 and #4
constrain external validity; #3 constrains measurement-validation grade; #5 constrains the upper
endpoint; #6, #8, and #13 require unusually visible methods disclosure; #11 is answered by the
construct distinction; and #12 and #14 constrain how the behavioural results may be described. None
of those defeats the narrower construct-validity claim when its population, epistemic grade, and
uncertainty layers are stated exactly - that claim rests on Studies 1-3, which involve no model call.

No frozen evidence can resolve human-coder transport, quantify model score inflation, or identify
the mechanism of the Study-4B capability shift. Those three limitations must remain limitations
rather than being argued away.
