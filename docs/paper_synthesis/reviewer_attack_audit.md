# Reviewer attack audit

**Document type:** frozen-artifact synthesis, not a preregistration.  
**Evidence cutoff:** Study 3 final freeze `8c39ad48f90f1369bf8729b3585cb17079349042`.

The audit assumes the paper makes the scoped measurement-validity claim in
`paper_architecture_v1.md`. It does not assume a Study-4 behavioral result.

| # | Strongest objection | Disposition | Evidence-backed response | Required paper discipline |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"This is just specification following, so what?"** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Following functional requirements is expected. The construct problem arises when a security benchmark is interpreted as measuring security behaviour distinct from compliance, while its visible prompt already obliges the scored security-oracle response. Study 1 shows this is common at the case level in SeCodePLT, and Study 3 shows that the obligation is often removable without losing capability determination. | Say the score is non-diagnostic between two constructs, not that specification following is undesirable or that every affected score is invalid. |
| 2 | **"You only studied SeCodePLT formally."** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Correct. Formal Definition-D prevalence is SeCodePLT-only. Other benchmarks contribute complementary structural evidence, and CWEval supplies a prospective portability failure, not a prevalence replication. | Keep all prevalence and separability claims benchmark- and eligibility-scoped; delete words such as "industry-wide" or "security benchmarks generally" from empirical claims. |
| 3 | **"The runs are LLM coding runs, not human coders."** | **Cannot be resolved in this paper** | The frozen evidence establishes reproducibility across independent blinded coding runs, including Study 2 cluster-aware kappa `0.804` and Study 1 internal kappa `0.8689`. Shared model/prompt lineage can still induce correlated error. | Use "coding runs" throughout. Treat human replication as a higher validation grade and an external limitation, not as something the current reliability statistics settle. |
| 4 | **"The 88.7% result is conditional on measured eligibility."** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Yes. Study 3's denominator is the realized measured-eligible confirmatory sample (`m=53`), not all SeCodePLT tasks. L2 separately records the eligibility sensitivity (`53` both-agree versus `56` either-agree). | Always write `47/53 of the realized measured-eligible confirmatory sample`; never call it whole-benchmark separability prevalence. Keep L2 separate from the headline. |
| 5 | **"VO=0 leaves a trivial upper bound, so the identification region is weak."** | **Already answered by frozen evidence** | The L0 region is `[47/53, 1]`. Its lower endpoint is highly informative about demonstrated existence; its upper endpoint is deliberately uninformative because VO-DEFECT was closed and VO-STRUCT established no certificate. Failure to establish VO is not evidence that obstruction is absent. | Present the lower-bound contribution and the unresolved upper side together. Do not turn `VO=0` into a nonexistence claim or hide the trivial upper endpoint. |
| 6 | **"Writer Run 1 failed, and Run 2 was repaired after failure."** | **Already answered by frozen evidence** | Run 1 is permanently preserved as `WRITER_INSTRUMENT_CONTRACT_FAILURE` and enters no classification. The repair boundary was frozen from mechanical contract issues without reading candidate substance; Run 2 used a fresh isolated session, the same 53-task universe and mapping, and became the sole formal constructive path. GAP-8 preserves the historical artifact while resolving provenance only. | Disclose the sequence in the main text and put exact manifests/diffs in the appendix. Call Run 2 an instrument-repaired replication, not the untouched first attempt. |
| 7 | **"You never showed that scores actually inflate."** | **Genuinely requires Study 4** | Correct. Studies 1-3 establish determination, reproducible measurement, and constructive removability. They do not estimate the causal effect of removing determination on model security-pass behaviour. | Retain "may conflate" or "cannot by itself distinguish"; forbid "inflates scores," "models exploit," or any magnitude claim. A causal model-execution study is the only direct answer. |
| 8 | **"The CWEval replication failed."** | **Already answered by frozen evidence** | It was attempted under a preregistered interface and terminated before outcomes when an outcome-blind audit showed that 83% of security cases required an unfrozen semantic extraction layer. The gate worked as designed and exposed non-portability of the measurement interface. | Report the termination prominently. Do not report CWEval Definition-D prevalence or use the mechanically convenient class-A subset as a substitute replication. |
| 9 | **"Definition D encodes your conclusion through subjective words like competent and obliged."** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | Definition D defines the target construct; it does not fix its empirical prevalence. It also makes positive judgments refutable: determined requires an identifiable quote, no external premise, and at most one inference step; undetermined is the tie-break default. Prospective blinded Study 2 and internal Study 1 reliability establish reproducibility, though reliability alone is not content validity. | Motivate why disclosed textual obligation is the relevant construct-validity boundary. Call Definition D an operational measurement with demonstrated run-level reliability, not a ground-truth semantic theorem, and acknowledge correlated-error and counterfactual-reader limitations. |
| 10 | **"Why is this security-specific rather than ordinary benchmark leakage?"** | **Answerable by wording/scope** | The mechanism is not uniquely security-specific. The security-specific validity problem comes from the intended contrast: capability requirements are normally meant to be specified, while a security score is often read as evidence of security behaviour beyond ordinary compliance. Study 1 deliberately reports `theta_cap` as a control rather than treating all determination as defective. | State that the general mechanism is requirement disclosure; the paper studies its consequences for interpreting a security subscore. Do not claim a uniquely security-only cognitive mechanism. |

## Residual-risk ordering

The only objection that changes the empirical claim class is #7: a claim about realized score
effects genuinely requires Study 4. Objections #2 and #4 constrain external validity; #3 constrains
measurement-validation grade; #5 constrains the upper endpoint; and #6/#8 require unusually visible
methods disclosure. None of those defeats the narrower construct-validity claim when its population,
epistemic grade, and uncertainty layers are stated exactly.

No frozen evidence can resolve human-coder transport or quantify model score inflation. Those two
limitations must remain limitations rather than being argued away.
